Reference Scale

All readings are based on photodiode ADC values measured in real time. Use this scale to understand your water quality output.

0 400 600 1023
High Microplastics Not Consummable Optimal & Pure
600 — 1023 ✓ Optimal

Optimal & Pure

Water is clean and safe for consumption. Photodiode detects high light transmission indicating low particle interference.

ADC Range 600 and above
400 — 599 ✕ Warning

Not Consummable

Water quality is below safe threshold. Moderate particle interference detected. Not recommended for drinking.

ADC Range 400 to 599
0 — 399 ⚠ Danger

High Microplastic Count

Dangerous levels of microplastics detected. High particle interference blocking light transmission significantly.

ADC Range Below 400

⚙ Values are based on photodiode ADC readings ranging from 0 to 1023. Higher values indicate clearer water with less particle interference.

Stay Safe

Purification Tips

If your water scan shows unsafe levels, here are the most effective ways to purify your water before consumption.

Boil Your Water

Bring water to a full rolling boil for at least 1 minute. This kills most bacteria, viruses and pathogens effectively.

Most Effective

Use a Micro Filter

Microfilters with pore sizes of 0.1–0.4 microns can physically remove microplastics and large contaminants from water.

Microplastics

Activated Carbon Filter

Carbon filters absorb chemicals, heavy metals and organic compounds improving taste, odor and overall water safety.

Chemical Removal

UV Purification

Ultraviolet light disrupts the DNA of microorganisms rendering them harmless. Highly effective against bacteria and viruses.

Bacteria & Viruses

Chlorination

Adding a small measured amount of chlorine to water kills most harmful microorganisms. Widely used in municipal water treatment.

Disinfection

Reverse Osmosis

Forces water through a semi-permeable membrane removing up to 99% of contaminants including microplastics, salts and heavy metals.

Deep Purification

Note — These tips are general guidelines. Always consult a water quality professional for severely contaminated water sources.
